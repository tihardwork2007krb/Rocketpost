Rocket Post - Offline Chat and Private Messaging

Rocket Post is a simple offline messaging project designed for private communication. Connect, share and chat with a simple and easy-to-use interface.

Rocket Post is designed with privacy and simple communication in mind. The current version stores chat information locally in the browser, making it useful for offline chat testing and personal communication.

Bluetooth-based device-to-device messaging is planned as a future feature for compatible mobile devices.

About Rocket Post

Rocket Post is a simple offline communication project designed to connect, share and chat.

It focuses on simple, private, offline communication.

Users can create conversations, send messages and keep their chat history stored locally on their device.
